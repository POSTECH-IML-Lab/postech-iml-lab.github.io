2 papers accepted to AISTATS! (i) Linear extension of the MED algorithm (or Maillard sampling) that is randomized, off-policy-friendly, and nearly optimal. (ii) Estimating the value of the maximum mean among K arms (rather than estimating who achieves it) that can surpass the performance of the oracle who knows the best arm and reports its sample mean!
