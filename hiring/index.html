We are actively recruiting self-motivated students for MS and PhD programs, as well as undergraduate research interns (preferably onsite). We generally prefer those with great communication skills.

What we work on

Besides what we listed in research, we are recently interested in scaling verification, training better reward/feedback models, conformal prediction, and RL with rich feedback.

Theory and practice

We are currently a theory-focused lab, but we are expanding to empirical research, so you do not have to be a theorist to join us. We value exploring how theoretically-derived algorithms work out in real-world problems, so even theory students will be working from modern ML setups to evaluate their algorithms.

Examples from our recent work: Colt’25 and Beyond RLHF.

Equipments

We have a large GPU cluster from GSAI. We also own a few RTX 6000 Pro GPUs (currently purchasing more).

What we offer undergraduate interns

  • Direct mentoring from the PI (weekly meetings of at least 30 minutes).
  • Hands-on research experience on high-end GPU servers
  • Support for submitting to and attending top-tier international conferences
  • Your own desk in the lab

How to apply

We accept applications year-round. Email the PI at kwangsungjun@postech.ac.kr with:

  • a short introduction
  • what and why you are interested (any format)
  • your transcript
